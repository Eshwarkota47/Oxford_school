FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Find quick answers regarding admission age, syllabus, bus facility, and fee schedules.

What is the age criteria for Kindergarten & Standard 1 admission?
For Nursery, the child must be 3+ years of age as of June 1st of the academic year. For LKG, 4+ years; UKG, 5+ years; and for Standard 1st, 6+ years as per the Karnataka Education Department guidelines.
Which curriculum and board is followed at Oxford English Medium School?
We follow the Karnataka State Board Curriculum enriched with NCERT-aligned science, mathematics, and digital smart class content. The medium of instruction is English, with Kannada and Hindi as language components.
Are school bus transportation facilities safe and available for nearby villages?
Yes! We have a dedicated fleet of GPS-tracked school buses covering Bukkapatna town, Tavarekere, Sira, Borasandra, Chelur, and surrounding rural routes. Each bus has an experienced licensed driver, assistant conductor, and lady attendant for student safety.
Can the annual school fee be paid in installments?
Yes, we provide the flexibility to pay the total academic and transport fee in three convenient term installments (Term 1 at admission in May/June, Term 2 in September, Term 3 in December).
What extracurricular activities are offered to students?
We offer weekly coaching in Karate (martial arts), Yoga & Meditation, Spoken English & Elocution, Science Club, Classical & Folk Dance, Drawing & Painting, Volleyball, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, and Athletics.
